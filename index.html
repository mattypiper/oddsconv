📐 Vig & Overround Calculation Guide

1. Implied Probabilities: Convert both sides' US odds to implied probabilities: P₁ and P₂.

2. Overround: The sum of both probabilities: Overround = P₁ + P₂ (typically > 100% due to bookmaker markup).

3. Vig / Hold Percentage: Vig = 1 - (1 / Overround) or (Overround - 1) / Overround. For -110 / -110, overround is 104.76%, yielding 4.54% vig.

4. Fair (No-Vig) Probabilities: Fair P₁ = P₁ / Overround and Fair P₂ = P₂ / Overround.
